Run your own toy company. Build the workshops, choose which customers to serve, and ship every toy by truck, train or sleigh. One small workshop grows into a busy snowy town over 150 levels.

Fair to play

Bonus videos are always your choice. There are no ads at all before level 8 and 30 minutes of play, no full-screen ads in your first session, and only a few in any session after that.
