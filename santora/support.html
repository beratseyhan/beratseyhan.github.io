Write to beratseyhan@icloud.com. Please add your device model, the game version and the Player ID shown in Settings, so we can help faster.

Will I lose my town if I change phones?

On iPhone and iPad your progress is copied to your own iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple ID and keep “iCloud sync” on in the game's settings, and your town comes back after a reinstall or on a new device. On Android the save stays on the device and may be included in your Google device backup.

I bought something and it is missing

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and town styles come back on any device that uses the same Apple ID or Google account. Gems are consumable and are delivered once. If something is still missing, e-mail us with the date of the purchase.

What does Remove Ads do?

It stops the banner and the full-screen ads for good. Bonus videos (for example double offline coins or a free boost) stay available, and watching them is always your choice.

A bonus video did not give me the reward

The reward is given when the video finishes. If you close the game during the video or the ad fails to load, no reward is given and nothing is taken from you; try again a little later. If it keeps happening, please tell us.

Reminders

The game can remind you when your daily gift is ready and when coins are waiting. Turn them on or off in the game's settings or in your device's notification settings.
