Contact

Write to beratseyhan@icloud.com. Please add your device model and the game version, so we can help faster. You do not need to send a Player ID: we cannot use it to look you up in any provider's system.

Questions about your data

Your save is on your device and, if iCloud sync is on, in your own iCloud. To reset it, open Settings in the game and, under Start Over, tap New Game; to remove it from the device, delete the app. The Privacy Policy explains how the iCloud copy is replaced. Analytics and ad data held by Firebase, Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads is handled by those providers; see the Privacy Policy for the details and for how to limit tracking or reset your advertising identifier. For anything else, e-mail us with the game version and what you need.

Will I lose my town if I change phones?

On iPhone and iPad your progress is copied to your own iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple ID and keep “iCloud sync” on in the game's settings, and your town comes back after a reinstall or on a new device.

I bought something and it is missing

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Remove Ads comes back on any device that uses the same Apple ID. The Starter Pack is a one-time offer: restoring it only marks the offer as used, and its gems and other items are not given a second time. Gems are consumable and are delivered once. If something is still missing, e-mail us with the date of the purchase.

What does Remove Ads do?

It stops the banner and the full-screen ads for good. Bonus videos (for example double offline coins or a free boost) stay available, and watching them is always your choice.

A bonus video did not give me the reward

The reward is given when the video finishes. If you close the game during the video or the ad fails to load, no reward is given and nothing is taken from you; try again a little later. If it keeps happening, please tell us.

Reminders

The game can remind you when your daily gift is ready, when coins are waiting and when the Night Express arrives. Turn them on or off in the game's settings or in your device's notification settings.
