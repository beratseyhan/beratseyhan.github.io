Who we are

Santora (shown in the stores as “Santora Idle Tycoon”) is made by Berat Seyhan, an independent developer. Contact: beratseyhan@icloud.com.

In short

No account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your progress stays on your device and, on iPhone and iPad, in your own iCloud. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay. To learn where players get stuck, the game sends play events, tied to a random app-instance ID rather than to your name, to Google Firebase Analytics. We run no servers of our own and we do not sell your personal information for money. Ad providers may use ad identifiers to show personalised ads; you control that with the tracking choices below.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your town, buildings, levels, coins and gems, settings (language, sound, reminders), what you have bought and a random Player ID. It stays on your device. On iPhone and iPad a copy is kept in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage) so it can come back after a reinstall or on a new device; we cannot read it. You can turn iCloud sync off in the game's settings. Deleting the app removes the save from the device, and Settings → Start Over resets it (see “Deleting your data” below).

Reminders (“your daily gift is ready”, “coins are waiting” and “the Night Express has arrived”) are scheduled on the device itself. Nothing is sent to a server, and you can turn them off in the game or in your device settings.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

The free game shows a banner on some menu screens and occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks (no banner or full-screen ads before level 8 and 30 minutes of play, and no full-screen ads in your first session), and bonus videos that you can choose to watch for a reward, at any time. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay by Unity Technologies and its subsidiary ironSource, from Unity Ads, ironSource Ads and other ad networks that LevelPlay works with (for example AppLovin, Mintegral, Liftoff/Vungle, InMobi and Meta Audience Network).

These providers may collect and process: your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), the identifier for vendors on iOS, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and operating-system information (such as model, OS version, language and network type), app information, the ads shown and your interactions with them, and diagnostic data of the ad software. They use it to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game does not ask for consent to personalised advertising, so it never gives it: when your device's region or time zone is in one of these countries, the game tells the ad providers that you have not consented, and you see only non-personalised ads. On iPhone and iPad, Apple's “Allow tracking?” question appears after the tutorial; if you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, your advertising identifier is not shared and the game works the same. The “Ad Privacy Choices” section in the game's settings opens the game's page in iOS Settings, where you can switch tracking on or off.

More: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy · ironSource privacy policy.

What the game and its providers collect

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), the identifier for vendors (IDFV), and the random app-instance and installation IDs that Firebase creates. Used for ads, analytics and keeping the game working.
  • Advertising data: ads shown and your interactions with them, including ad revenue figures. Used for ads, measurement and analytics.
  • Product interaction: screens, sessions and play events (tutorial, levels, shop, ads). Used for analytics.
  • Purchase history: which products you bought and when, through Apple. Used for analytics.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address (region or country, never GPS). Used for ads and analytics.
  • Diagnostics: performance, crash and device statistics from the ad SDK and the Unity engine (see “Game engine”). Used to keep the game stable.

This data is tied to your device by the identifiers above, not to your name. We do not collect contact details, user content, financial information or precise location.

In-app purchases

Purchases (gems, Remove Ads and the Starter Pack) are processed by Apple (App Store) under Apple's own privacy policy. We never receive your payment details. The game receives the product and a transaction identifier so it can deliver the item and restore it later (Settings → Restore Purchases). “Remove Ads” stops the banner and the full-screen ads; bonus videos stay available and are always optional.

Game analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To see where new players get stuck and whether a level is too hard, the game sends play events to Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): for example tutorial steps, levels reached, minutes played, ads watched, purchases made (product and price) and offline rewards claimed. Firebase also records a random app-instance ID, device model, operating-system version, app version, language, country and session times. It does not receive your name or contact details. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland these events are not used for advertising. Firebase keeps user-level and event data for the retention period set in our Google Analytics settings, which is 2 months (the shortest option; the maximum Google offers is 14 months), and then deletes it. Aggregated reports without identifiers can be kept longer. See Firebase privacy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Game engine

Santora is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies receives technical and diagnostic information about the device and the game (such as model, operating system, processor, graphics hardware, screen size, performance and crash reports) to improve the engine; Unity Engine diagnostics are switched on in this game. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Children

Santora is a game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in some EEA countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Ads and tracking. On iPhone and iPad, choose “Ask App Not to Track” at Apple's prompt, or switch tracking off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; the game then does not share your advertising identifier and works the same. You can also reset or limit ad tracking in iOS settings. Under some US state laws, sharing identifiers with ad providers for personalised ads can count as “sale” or “sharing”; refusing tracking stops that for the advertising identifier. The game does not offer a separate “do not sell” switch.

Deleting your data

We run no server and no account system, so we hold no profile about you and have no Player ID or account to look up. The random Player ID in your save stays on your device (and in your iCloud copy) and is never sent to Firebase or to the ad providers.

  1. Game save: open Settings in the game and, under Start Over, tap New Game. This resets your town and progress; purchases and settings are kept so you can restore them. With iCloud sync on, the copy in your own iCloud is replaced by the fresh game once you have played it for a few minutes and the game syncs; if another device on the same Apple ID has newer progress, that progress comes back instead, so start over there as well. Turning iCloud sync off stops new uploads but does not remove the copy already in iCloud. Deleting the app removes everything the game stored on the device.
  2. Analytics data (Firebase): events are tied to a random app-instance ID that the game does not show, so we cannot find your records. They are deleted automatically after the 2-month retention period above, and deleting the app stops new collection from that device.
  3. Ad data (Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and connected networks): this data is held by those providers under their own policies and deletion processes (see the links in the Advertising section). To cut it off from your side, refuse tracking or reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security → Tracking and Apple Advertising); a new identifier is not linked to the old one.

Questions or requests about your data: e-mail beratseyhan@icloud.com from your own address with the game version and the country of your App Store account. You do not need to send a Player ID. We will answer and explain what we and each provider can and cannot delete; we cannot match you to records held by the providers ourselves.
