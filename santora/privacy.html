Who we are

Santora (shown in the stores as “Santora Idle Tycoon”) is made by Berat Seyhan, an independent developer. Contact: beratseyhan@icloud.com.

In short

No account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your progress stays on your device and, on iPhone and iPad, in your own iCloud. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay. To learn where players get stuck, the game sends anonymous play events to Google Firebase Analytics. We run no servers of our own and do not sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your town, buildings, levels, coins and gems, settings (language, sound, reminders), what you have bought and a random Player ID. It stays on your device. On iPhone and iPad a copy is kept in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage) so it can come back after a reinstall or on a new device; we cannot read it. You can turn iCloud sync off in the game's settings. Deleting the app or using Settings → New Game removes the save from the device.

Reminders (“your daily gift is ready”, “coins are waiting”) are scheduled on the device itself. Nothing is sent to a server, and you can turn them off in the game or in your device settings.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

The free game shows a banner on some menu screens and occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks (no ads at all before level 8 and 30 minutes of play, and no full-screen ads in your first session), and bonus videos that you can choose to watch for a reward. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay by Unity Technologies and its subsidiary ironSource, from Unity Ads, ironSource Ads and other ad networks that LevelPlay works with (for example AppLovin, Mintegral, Liftoff/Vungle, InMobi and Meta Audience Network).

These providers may collect and process: your device's advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking; on Android you can reset or delete it in Android's settings), the identifier for vendors on iOS, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and operating-system information (such as model, OS version, language and network type), app information, the ads shown and your interactions with them, and diagnostic data of the ad software. They use it to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game does not ask for consent to personalised advertising, so it never gives it: when your device's region or time zone is in one of these countries, the game tells the ad providers that you have not consented, and you see only non-personalised ads. On iPhone and iPad, Apple's “Allow tracking?” question appears after the tutorial; if you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, your advertising identifier is not shared and the game works the same. “Ad Privacy Choices” in the game's settings opens the ad provider's own privacy options where available.

More: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy · ironSource privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases (gems, Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and town styles) are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never receive your payment details. The game receives the product and a transaction identifier so it can deliver the item and restore it later (Settings → Restore Purchases). “Remove Ads” stops the banner and the full-screen ads; bonus videos stay available and are always optional.

Game analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To see where new players get stuck and whether a level is too hard, the game sends play events to Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): for example tutorial steps, levels reached, minutes played, ads watched, purchases made (product and price) and offline rewards claimed. Firebase also records a random app-instance ID, device model, operating-system version, app version, language, country and session times. It does not receive your name or contact details. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland these events are not used for advertising. Firebase keeps this data for up to 14 months. See Firebase privacy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your coin total on the leaderboard is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Game engine

Santora is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may receive anonymous technical information about the device (such as model, operating system, processor, graphics hardware and screen size) to improve the engine. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Children

Santora is a game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in some EEA countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. We hold no personal data about you on a server of our own. To ask for data linked to your device to be deleted at Firebase, e-mail us the Player ID shown in Settings and we will forward the request. For ad data, use your device's tracking and advertising settings or contact Unity through the policy linked above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date below will change.
